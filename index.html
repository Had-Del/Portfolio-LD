2023 · Horror / Investigation · Vertical slice · PC

In'Sect

Level Design

A crypto-zoologist explores the ruins of a temple built by a cult on a mountaintop, searching for the truth about his parents.

Mechanics, constraints and solutions

Mechanics

Exploration, stealth in hostile shadows, and puzzles unlocked with the player's phone.

Constraints

Navigating an enclosed building, and building levels while the game design kept evolving: a level could work one week and be unusable the next.

Solutions

A constant QA loop on every segment (try, feedback, modify, evolve or cut). When changes piled up, level design paused so the team could agree on new features and their impact first.

2022 · Action-adventure FPS · Blockout · PC

The Be[a]st Show

Level Design · Lighting

In 2123, an activist infiltrates a deadly TV show. His gravity gun is his only edge, in a game that alternates action and narrative phases.

Mechanics, constraints and solutions

Mechanics

Switch the gravity of a room (up, down, zero) or of a single enemy, plus run, jump and shoot.

Constraints

Using gravity as a weapon in a free-for-all arena, and making rooms playable on both floor and ceiling.

Solutions

Kill with the environment: in a forge factory, molten metal, steam vents and presses become traps. Each room was designed twice, with different but coherent patterns, so the floor becomes the ceiling.

2022 · Action-adventure · Blockout · PC

Fortress

Level Design · Narrative Design

A corsair during the War of the Spanish Succession reaches a French fortress after an English assault, looking for a black powder ship.

Mechanics, constraints and solutions

Mechanics

Jump, climb, interact. The focus was environmental storytelling, so no complex features.

Constraints

Telling an understandable post-battle story through the level, around an NPC who is as important as the player.

Solutions

Keep the fortress and its aftermath, reinforce the NPC's traits and make the mission satirical rather than serious, to leave a memorable moment.

2022 · Action-adventure · Blockout · PC · 3-week workshop

Jedi Fallen Order: Kamino

Level Design · Level Building · Lighting

A level set after the end of the game: rescue scientists on Kamino and counter the 4th Sister.

Mechanics, constraints and solutions

Mechanics

The character's core movement (jump, double jump, attacks, dodge, climb) plus an astral phase that lets the player pass through some surfaces, and one-way high-speed tubes.

Constraints

Making the new mechanic matter: backtracking, collectibles reachable only in astral form, and “constrain without blocking, discover without over-guiding”. Enemy AI also made some areas too hard.

Solutions

Several rooms were rebuilt from scratch around the astral gameplay, and the AI was tested and rebalanced.

2021–2022 · Horror / Investigation · Vertical slice · PC

Cryptids

Level Design · Level Building · Environment (foliage)

An investigator explores an abandoned Amish village in an Ontario forest and must bring back proof of a creature: a photograph.

Mechanics, constraints and solutions

Mechanics

Exploration, avoiding the creature, collecting clues, photographing it, optionally neutralizing it for a better reward, then escaping.

Constraints

Creating fear on a semi-open map, where the player can go almost anywhere and the path is hard to anticipate.

Solutions

Guiding with light and darkness, roots and trees. It was only a partial solution: the golden path and flow were not fully optimized, which was the key lesson of the project.

2021–2022 · Co-op escape game · Blockout · PC

Key to Freedom

Level Design · Lighting · Narrative Design

Survivors team up to escape a house of death run by a psychopathic millionaire: an escape game crossed with a thriller. My end-of-year personal project.

Mechanics, constraints and solutions

Mechanics

Interacting with the scenery and with other players, running, and using items to survive.

Constraints

Making player-to-player communication easy, and keeping an escape game replayable.

Solutions

A proximity chat and a ping system for communication. For replayability: more puzzles than needed, fixed room positions with varying content, and several exit doors of which only one unlocks.

About me

I'm Hadrien, a junior Level / Game Designer based in Lyon. I grew up on Age of Empires, The Elder Scrolls and The Witcher (the first Witcher is underrated), and my goal is simple: work in the industry and make games I'm proud of.

I'm now completing a Master's in innovation project management and entrepreneurship, which adds team coordination, planning and delivery follow-up to my design background.

Soft skills. Curious, with a wide view of a project and its parts. Attentive: every idea carries inspiration, and every piece of feedback is a way to improve. Diplomatic: listening only matters if we can build something with what we hear.

Hard skills

  • Maya for props, foliage, lighting
